Personal Reflections

This short self-assessment looks at your everyday emotional patterns and your professional resilience. Answer each question honestly with a simple Yes or No — there are no right or wrong answers. At the end you'll see your own results.

  • 25 quick Yes/No questions — about 4–5 minutes.
  • Your answers stay on your device — you can save the results as a PDF for yourself.
  • Whatever you find, it's information for your own reflection — not a verdict.

Keep a copy

Print this page or save it as a PDF to revisit your results later.

This tool is for personal reflection and education only. It is not a medical or psychological diagnosis and does not replace care from a qualified professional. If you are in crisis, please reach out using the resources shown with your results.
